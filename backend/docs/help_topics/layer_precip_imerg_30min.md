# Rain right now (IMERG 30-minute)

id: layer_precip_imerg_30min
short: Satellite rain for the latest half hour, over land and sea.

## What it is
NASA's GPM IMERG Early estimate, 30-minute average, about 4-6 hours behind.

## How to read it
mm per hour, scale 0.1 to 53: pale = light rain (<1 mm/h), strongest colours = downpours (>10-20 mm/h).

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Shows storm bands approaching Samui from the sea where no radar reaches.

## Limits
Satellite estimate: can miss short island showers or overestimate some storms.

## Sources
- https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/imerg
