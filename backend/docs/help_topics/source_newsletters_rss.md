## What it is
The Climate Brink (climate scientists Zeke Hausfather and Andrew Dessler) writes regularly about El Nino and global temperature records; Yale Climate Connections and Climate Signals publish climate news and explainers.

## How to read it
The Climate Brink: posts about El Nino or its impacts. The other two: El Nino posts only. Tagged 'newsletter'.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Expert context on what this El Nino means globally (record heat, a glimpse of a warmer future) beyond the raw numbers.

## Limits
Opinion and analysis, not forecasts. Weeks can pass without an El Nino post.
