# Land surface temperature (night)

id: layer_land_temp_night
short: Night-time ground temperature.

## What it is
MODIS Terra night pass (about 10:30 pm).

## How to read it
Kelvin: 295 K = 22 degC, 300 K = 27 degC.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Warm nights make heat waves dangerous for health.

## Limits
Gaps where clouds cover the ground.

## Sources
- https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/mod11.php
