# Exit routes: flights

id: exit_route_air
short: Flights from Samui airport (USM) and from Surat Thani airport (URT) on the mainland.

## What it is
Samui International Airport (most flights by Bangkok Airways; Thai Airways also sells Bangkok-Samui). Surat Thani airport, reached after the ferry, has more airlines and seats (e.g. Nok Air, AirAsia).

## How to read it
Check seats and flight status on the airline sites the same day. Bangkok Airways' site blocks automated checks, so open it in a browser.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
The airport stays open when ferries stop for waves, but seats are few and expensive when everyone leaves; storms can close it too.

## Limits
No schedules or fares are given. Pets and large luggage have airline-specific rules.
