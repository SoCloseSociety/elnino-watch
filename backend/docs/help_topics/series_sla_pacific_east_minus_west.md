# Pacific sea level tilt (east minus west)

id: series_sla_pacific_east_minus_west
short: sla_pacific_east_minus_west = sla_nino34 minus sla_west_pacific.

## What it is
The difference between the sea level in the central-east equatorial Pacific and in the western Pacific warm pool, in cm.

## How to read it
Positive and large = the Pacific is tilted the El Nino way (water piled up in the east). On 2026-09-22 it was about +45 cm. Near zero or negative = neutral or La Nina.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Global sea level rise is in both boxes and cancels out, so this is a clean ENSO signal that reacts to wind changes within days.

## Limits
A simple two-box difference, not an official index.
