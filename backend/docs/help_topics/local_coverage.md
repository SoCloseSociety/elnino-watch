# Data coverage and the 'Unknown' level

id: local_coverage
short: The island level is only given when every critical factor has current data.

## What it is
Six factors are critical: El Nino, water/rain, heat, heavy rain/flood, cyclone/wind and sea state. `coverage` lists how many factors have data, which are missing, which are stale, and which critical ones are missing.

## How to read it
If one critical factor has no data or only old data, the overall level shows 'Unknown' instead of a number, and the headline names what is missing. 'Unknown' is not 'Normal': it means the app cannot tell. The phrase 'No immediate threat on Samui' only appears when all critical data is present and fresh.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
A wrong 'Normal' could make you skip preparing or miss the moment to leave. When a source is down, it is safer to say so plainly and point you to the TMD and ferry operators directly.

## Limits
Coverage says whether data arrived, not whether the data is right. Model forecasts can still be wrong even when fresh.
