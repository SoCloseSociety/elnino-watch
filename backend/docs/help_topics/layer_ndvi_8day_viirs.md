# Vegetation greenness (NDVI, 8-day)

id: layer_ndvi_8day_viirs
short: How green plants are, over a rolling 8 days.

## What it is
Normalized Difference Vegetation Index from VIIRS on NOAA-20.

## How to read it
0 = bare ground to 0.8-0.9 = dense green. Browner than usual in farmland = drought stress.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Drought shows up in rice and fruit areas of Thailand weeks before crop reports.

## Limits
Clouds leave gaps; compare with earlier dates, not with other regions.
