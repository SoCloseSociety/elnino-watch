# NASA POWER vs ERA5 cross-check

id: status_samui_power_crosscheck
short: How closely NASA POWER and Open-Meteo ERA5 agree for Samui, day by day.

## What it is
For the days both have, the mean difference and mean absolute difference of daily maximum and minimum temperature and rain between NASA POWER and ERA5.

## How to read it
Small mean differences (under 1 degC) mean the two reanalyses agree. On 2026-09-24: max temperature +0.6 degC on average over 55 days; rain POWER 290 mm vs ERA5 189 mm.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
When two independent sources agree, a drought or heat signal is more trustworthy.

## Limits
Both are coarse models; neither is a rain gauge on the island.
