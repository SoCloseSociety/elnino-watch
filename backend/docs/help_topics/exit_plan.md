# Exit plan

id: exit_plan
short: Whether and when you can leave the island, how, and what to do before going.

## What it is
Combines the island risk level with sea, wind, cyclone and rain signals, a 7-day window of crossing conditions, the verified exit routes and a departure checklist.

## How to read it
Start with the recommendation line. Signals show ok / caution / blocked / unknown. The windows list the next 7 local days; 'ok' means crossing conditions look normal. Routes link to the operators' official sites: check the day's departures there.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Samui can only be left by boat or plane. Boats stop when the sea rises, often before the worst weather: leaving early is the safe choice.

## Limits
No schedules or prices are shown because they change. Model forecasts can miss local squalls. 'Unknown' means missing data, never safe.
