# NOAA global and Asia monthly anomalies

id: series_ncei_anomalies
short: ncei_global_anom and ncei_asia_land_anom.

## What it is
NOAA NCEI monthly temperature departures for the globe (land + ocean) and Asian land.

## How to read it
degC above 1901-2000 (an older baseline, so numbers look higher than NASA's).

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Ranks the El Nino year for the globe and for Asia.

## Limits
Monthly; continental values are land only.

- https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/monitoring/climate-at-a-glance/global/time-series
