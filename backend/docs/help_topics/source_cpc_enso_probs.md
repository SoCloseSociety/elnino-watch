# CPC ENSO probabilities and strength

id: source_cpc_enso_probs
short: NOAA's official monthly odds of El Nino, neutral or La Nina, and of how strong El Nino will be, for the next 9 seasons.

## What it is
Each month (2nd Thursday) a team at NOAA's Climate Prediction Center publishes two tables. The first gives the chance of La Nina, neutral and El Nino for 9 overlapping 3-month seasons (ASO = Aug-Sep-Oct, SON, ...). The second splits those chances into strength classes, measured by the relative Nino 3.4 index (RONI).

## How to read it
Numbers are percentages and each row adds up to 100. Strength classes by the index value: weak +0.5 to +1.0 C, moderate +1.0 to +1.5 C, strong +1.5 to +2.0 C, very strong at or above +2.0 C (the same steps for La Nina below zero). Red bars = El Nino, grey = neutral, blue = La Nina. In September 2026: 97% chance of a very strong El Nino for Sep-Nov.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
The stronger the El Nino, the more likely its typical effects in Thailand: a weaker, later rainy season, then a hotter and drier dry season (Feb-May), water shortages on Samui, haze and coral bleaching. The later seasons show when NOAA expects the event to end.

## Limits
Strength of the index does not guarantee strength of the local impact. The outlook is a human consensus of models, updated only monthly. 0% is rounded: it means very unlikely, not impossible.
