# Vegetation greenness (NDVI, monthly)

id: layer_ndvi_monthly_modis
short: Cloud-free monthly greenness.

## What it is
MODIS Terra monthly NDVI, dated the 1st of the month.

## How to read it
0 to 1: brown = sparse or stressed, dark green = dense. Compare the same month across years.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Shows the cumulative effect of an El Nino dry season on vegetation.

## Limits
About 1-2 months behind.

- https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/mod13.php
