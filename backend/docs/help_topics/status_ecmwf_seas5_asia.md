# ECMWF Asia outlook maps

id: status_ecmwf_seas5_asia
short: Latest ECMWF 3-month rain and temperature outlook maps for Asia.

## What it is
Status key ecmwf_seas5_asia {base_time, charts: [{id, kind, season, url, how_to_read}], climate_period}.

## How to read it
Brown = drier favoured, green = wetter, white = no signal (rain); orange/red warmer, blue colder (temperature). Darker = more likely.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Tells whether the monsoon and dry season ahead lean dry or hot over southern Thailand.

## Limits
Monthly; probabilities, not amounts.
