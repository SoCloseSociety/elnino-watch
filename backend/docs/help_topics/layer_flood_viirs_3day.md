# Flood water (VIIRS 3-day)

id: layer_flood_viirs_3day
short: Land under water that is not normally flooded, last 3 days.

## What it is
NASA LANCE flood product from the VIIRS instruments on NOAA-20 and NOAA-21.

## How to read it
Red = flood, yellow = recurring seasonal flood, light blue = normal water, grey = not enough clear views (clouds).

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Monsoon floods in southern Thailand (Nakhon Si Thammarat, Surat Thani) cut roads and ferries' feeder routes.

## Limits
Clouds hide floods; no red does not prove no flooding. 375 m pixels miss small floods.
