# Exit signal: waves

id: exit_signal_waves
short: Forecast maximum wave height off Samui, today and the next 2 days.

## What it is
Daily maximum significant wave height from the Open-Meteo marine model, for open water east of the island.

## How to read it
Below 2 m: ok. 2 to 3 m: caution (catamarans and small boats are cancelled first). 3 m or more: blocked (car ferries likely suspended). No recent forecast: unknown.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
The ferries to Donsak and the catamarans to Chumphon are the main way off the island.

## Limits
A model value for one grid point; the Donsak ferries cross the more sheltered southwest side, the Chumphon catamaran the open Gulf. Operators decide cancellations themselves.
