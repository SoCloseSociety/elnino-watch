# Agency videos (YouTube)

id: source_youtube_channels
short: New El Nino videos from NOAA, NASA, WMO, Copernicus and The Weather Channel, and impact videos from Thai PBS.

## What it is
The app reads each channel's public YouTube RSS feed. International channels: only videos that mention El Nino / ENSO. Thai PBS (Thailand's public broadcaster, in Thai): videos about El Nino or its impacts (flood, drought, storm, heat, haze).

## How to read it
Each item links to the video and carries the tag 'video'. Titles stay in their original language.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Short explainers from the agencies that produce the data, and Thai television coverage of local impacts.

## Limits
Only the latest 15 videos of each channel are visible in the RSS feed. Each channel's identity is checked on every run so a wrong channel is never shown.
