# Samui last 30 days vs normal (NASA POWER)

id: status_samui_power
short: Rain total and mean temperature of the last 30 days compared with 2001-2020.

## What it is
Built from NASA POWER: 30-day rain total, the normal total for the same days, the percentage of normal, and the mean temperature with its normal and anomaly.

## How to read it
precip_pct_of_normal below ~60 % for several weeks in October-December would be an El Nino-type dry monsoon. At 2026-09-21: 186 mm (111 % of normal), 28.7 degC (+0.7).

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
The island's water supply depends on the October-December rains.

## Limits
Grid-cell values; the normals are 2001-2020, not 1991-2020.
