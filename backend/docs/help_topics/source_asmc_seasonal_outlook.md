# ASEAN seasonal outlook (ASMC)

id: source_asmc_seasonal_outlook
short: The regional climate centre's outlook for rain, temperature and haze in South-East Asia for the coming month and season.

## What it is
The ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre (a WMO Regional Climate Centre) publishes early each month a multi-model outlook for the ASEAN region, with the El Nino context and the smoke-haze risk. We keep the overview, the 'Mainland South-East Asia' paragraphs and the maps.

## How to read it
Terms: below-normal / near-normal / above-normal = the lower, middle or upper third of past years. 'Model skill' says how much to trust the forecast for that area.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
The official regional view of what El Nino means for rain and haze in Thailand for the next 3 months. September 2026 issue: below-normal rainfall and above-normal temperature for most of the region.

## Limits
Regional, not specific to Samui; national services (TMD) have the final word. Updated once a month.
