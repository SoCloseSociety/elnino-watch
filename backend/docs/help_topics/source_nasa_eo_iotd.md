# NASA Earth Observatory stories

id: source_nasa_eo_iotd
short: NASA's daily satellite image story, kept only when it is about El Nino impacts.

## What it is
NASA Earth Observatory publishes one explained satellite image per working day. The app keeps the ones about El Nino, drought, heat, fire and haze, floods, storms, coral bleaching, the oceans or Southeast Asia.

## How to read it
Each item links to the article; titles stay as published.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Good plain-language explanations with pictures of what El Nino does around the world.

## Limits
Only the last ~10 stories are in the feed; many days have no matching story, which is normal.
