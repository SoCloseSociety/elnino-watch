# Global sea surface temperature, daily (ERA5, 60S-60N)

id: series_era5_sst_6060
short: era5_sst_6060 and era5_sst_6060_anom.

## What it is
Daily mean sea surface temperature between 60S and 60N from Copernicus Climate Pulse, and its difference from 1991-2020.

## How to read it
degC; around 21.0 degC and +0.7 in late September 2026.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Ocean heat drives air temperature and coral bleaching risk; El Nino raises it.

## Limits
Last 2-3 days preliminary. Not the same dataset as the OISST world SST series.

## Sources
- https://pulse.climate.copernicus.eu/
