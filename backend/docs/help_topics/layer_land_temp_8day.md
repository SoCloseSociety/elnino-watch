# Land surface temperature (8-day)

id: layer_land_temp_8day
short: Daytime ground temperature averaged over 8 days.

## What it is
MODIS Terra 8-day land surface temperature.

## How to read it
Kelvin: 300 K = 27 degC, 310 K = 37 degC, 320 K = 47 degC.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Shows heat building over Thai farmland and towns with fewer cloud gaps than daily data.

## Limits
Ground skin temperature, hotter than air in the sun; about 10 days behind.

- https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/mod11.php
