# Night lights (VIIRS Day/Night Band)

id: layer_night_lights_viirs
short: Last night's lights seen from space.

## What it is
Radiance measured by the VIIRS Day/Night Band on NOAA-20 around 1:30 am.

## How to read it
nW/cm2/sr from 0 to about 38: brighter = more light. Compare the same place on several clear nights.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
After a storm, a town that goes dark on a clear night suggests a power cut.

## Limits
Clouds block the view; moonlight brightens clouds and sea.
