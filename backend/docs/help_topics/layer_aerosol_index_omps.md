# Smoke and dust index (OMPS)

id: layer_aerosol_index_omps
short: Ultraviolet index of smoke and dust, visible even above clouds.

## What it is
UV aerosol index from the OMPS instrument on NOAA-21.

## How to read it
Below 0 to 5 and above: over ~1 = smoke or dust present, over 2-3 = thick smoke.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
El Nino dry seasons bring fire haze from Sumatra and Borneo that can reach the Gulf of Thailand.

## Limits
Coarse (~50 km); does not give ground-level air quality (see PM2.5 series).
