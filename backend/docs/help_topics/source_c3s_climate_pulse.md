# Copernicus Climate Pulse

id: source_c3s_climate_pulse
short: Europe's daily global air and sea temperature, with the difference from 1991-2020.

## What it is
The Copernicus Climate Change Service publishes daily global averages from the ERA5 reanalysis: air temperature at 2 m over the whole planet and sea surface temperature between 60S and 60N.

## How to read it
Values in degC plus the anomaly vs 1991-2020. On 2026-09-22: air 16.06 degC (+0.78), sea 21.03 degC (+0.71). The last 2-3 days are marked preliminary and can change slightly.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Global records usually follow a strong El Nino by a few months. This is the fastest official global thermometer (2 days behind).

## Limits
Global averages hide regional detail. The sea series is ERA5, not the OISST series shown elsewhere; small differences between them are normal.
