# ReliefWeb humanitarian reports

id: source_reliefweb_reports
short: UN and NGO situation reports that mention El Nino or concern Thailand.

## What it is
ReliefWeb (UN OCHA) collects reports, appeals and assessments from humanitarian organisations. The app reads those of the last 60 days that mention El Nino or are about Thailand.

## How to read it
Each item is an official report with its publishing organisations. Tags mark El Nino and Thailand items.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Shows how El Nino is affecting food, water and health in the region, and whether Thailand has an active disaster response.

## Limits
Needs a free approved 'appname' from ReliefWeb (set RELIEFWEB_APPNAME); without it the source shows 'needs configuration'.
