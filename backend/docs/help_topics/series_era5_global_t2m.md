# Global air temperature, daily (ERA5)

id: series_era5_global_t2m
short: era5_global_t2m and era5_global_t2m_anom.

## What it is
Daily global mean air temperature at 2 m from Copernicus Climate Pulse, and its difference from 1991-2020.

## How to read it
degC. The anomaly is what matters: around +0.8 in September 2026.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
The planet usually sets new daily records a few months after a strong El Nino peaks.

## Limits
Last 2-3 days preliminary (meta.status).

## Sources
- https://pulse.climate.copernicus.eu/
