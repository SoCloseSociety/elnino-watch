# ECMWF seasonal outlook maps for Asia

id: source_ecmwf_seas5_asia
short: Europe's 3-month rain and temperature outlook maps for Asia, updated monthly.

## What it is
ECMWF's SEAS5 seasonal model runs 51 times each month. The app keeps its open outlook maps for Asia: rain for the next 4 overlapping 3-month seasons and temperature for the next 2.

## How to read it
Each map shows the most likely category vs 1993-2016 and how likely it is. Rain: brown/orange = drier than normal favoured (darker = more likely, up to 70-100 %), green/teal = wetter, white = no clear signal. Temperature: orange/red = warmer, blue = colder. The September 2026 run favours a dry OND 2026 over the Philippines and the South China Sea.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Samui's main rains fall October-December; a drier outlook there means water shortages later into 2027.

## Limits
Probabilities, not amounts. Images only (numbers are not open). The Nino plume from ECMWF is a separate source (ecmwf_nino_plume).
