# What the price range means

id: shopping_price_range
short: The cheapest to the dearest listing seen on the date shown, for the quantity on your checklist rounded up to whole packs. Indicative only: prices change and delivery is not included.
category: prep
related: shopping_budget, shopping_panel, freshness

## What it is
Each price was read by hand on a shop page on the "checked" date and saved with its link. For your household every listing becomes a cost: packs needed = your quantity divided by what one pack holds, rounded up; cost = packs x pack price. The range runs from the cheapest listing to the dearest. Example: 112 L of drinking water (2 people x 4 L x 14 days): 6 L bottles at 35 THB need 19 bottles = 665 THB; 9 L packs at 40 THB need 13 packs = 520 THB.

## How to read it
"4.44-6.33 THB per L" is a unit price; "520-722 THB" is what your quantity costs. A price marked "marketplace seller" came from a Lazada or Shopee seller listed on Priceza: it varies more and fakes are more likely. Food per person-day uses a stated assumption shown under the item.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
A range is more honest than one number; plan with the upper value to be safe.

## Limits
Delivery, installation and fuel are not included. After 60 days prices are flagged "re-check".
