# Exit routes: ferries

id: exit_route_ferry
short: Boats from Samui to the mainland.

## What it is
Car and passenger ferries: Seatran Ferry (Nathon to Donsak) and Raja Ferry (Lipa Noi to Donsak). Lomprayah: boat + bus to Donsak / Surat Thani, and high-speed catamaran to Chumphon via Koh Phangan and Koh Tao.

## How to read it
Check the day's departures and cancellations on the operator's site or at the pier. Car ferries are the most weather-resistant; the Chumphon catamaran is the most exposed.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Ferries carry most people, vehicles and pets off the island and stop first in rough seas.

## Limits
No schedules or fares are given: they change. Only official websites that answered on 2026-09-24 are linked.
