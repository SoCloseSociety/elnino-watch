# NOAA monthly climate reports

id: source_ncei_monthly_report
short: NOAA's monthly global climate, global drought and tropical cyclone reports.

## What it is
Every month NOAA NCEI publishes 'State of the Climate' reports. The app keeps the global ones: temperature and rain, drought, and tropical cyclones.

## How to read it
Each item has the headline numbers and a link to the full report.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Expert summaries that mention ENSO's role and regional extremes, including Asia.

## Limits
Monthly; the national (US) reports are skipped.
