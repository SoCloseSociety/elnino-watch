# NOAA Climate at a Glance

id: source_ncei_cag
short: NOAA's official monthly temperature departures for the globe and for Asia.

## What it is
NOAA's National Centers for Environmental Information publish monthly anomalies from their NOAAGlobalTemp dataset for the globe (land + ocean) and for each continent.

## How to read it
degC above the 1901-2000 average (a different, older baseline than NASA's and the Met Office's, so the numbers are higher). August 2026: globe +1.32, Asian land +1.75.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Shows how the El Nino year ranks against the record for the globe and for Asia.

## Limits
Monthly, around the 10th of the next month. Continental values are for land only.
