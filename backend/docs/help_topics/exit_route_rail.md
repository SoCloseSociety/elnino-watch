# Exit routes: train from Surat Thani

id: exit_route_rail
short: Trains from Surat Thani station (Phun Phin) on the mainland.

## What it is
The State Railway of Thailand's southern line links Surat Thani with Bangkok and the rest of the network; tickets are sold online (D-Ticket).

## How to read it
Useful after the ferry when flights are full. Check the train and seats on the SRT sites.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
A high-capacity onward option once you are on the mainland.

## Limits
Floods in the South can cut the line during the northeast monsoon; check before relying on it.
