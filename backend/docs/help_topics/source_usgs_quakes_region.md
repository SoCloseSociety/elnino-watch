# Regional earthquakes (USGS)

id: source_usgs_quakes_region
short: Magnitude 4.5+ earthquakes of the last 7 days within 3000 km of Koh Samui.

## What it is
The USGS global feed of M4.5+ earthquakes, filtered to Sumatra, the Andaman Sea, Myanmar, the Philippines and Indonesia.

## How to read it
Severity: red = magnitude 7 or more, or the USGS tsunami flag; orange = magnitude 6 or more; green = smaller. The flag means the event is in an area where tsunamis are possible, not that one happened.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Not an El Nino effect. It is here so that a large regional quake is never missed: the 2004 Sumatra tsunami hit Thailand's Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi, Khao Lak). Koh Samui is in the Gulf of Thailand, well sheltered from Indian Ocean tsunamis.

## Limits
Magnitudes are revised in the first hours. For tsunami warnings in Thailand follow the NDWC / TMD, not this list.
