# Exit signal: heavy rain

id: exit_signal_rain
short: Forecast heavy rain and TMD heavy-rain bulletins for the South.

## What it is
Maximum daily rain forecast for Samui (today + 2 days) and any TMD heavy-rain bulletin in force for the South / Gulf.

## How to read it
A TMD bulletin or 90 mm or more in a day: caution (flooded roads to the piers and on the mainland). 150 mm or more: blocked.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Leaving by ferry means driving to the pier and on through Surat Thani, where roads and the railway can flood in the monsoon.

## Limits
Rain forecasts are uncertain at this scale; a TMD bulletin covers a large area, not only Samui.
