# Sea surface salinity (SMAP)

id: layer_sea_salinity_smap
short: Saltiness of the sea surface, 8-day average.

## What it is
NASA SMAP radiometer, JPL CAP algorithm, 8-day running mean.

## How to read it
PSU, scale 30-40; open ocean ~34-35. Fresher = heavy rain or river water.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
During El Nino the fresh water under the Pacific rain band moves east; in the Gulf of Thailand fresher water follows big rain and river floods.

## Limits
Less reliable within ~40 km of coasts (so around Samui itself).
