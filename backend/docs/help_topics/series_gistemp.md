# GISTEMP global and tropical anomaly

id: series_gistemp
short: gistemp_global_anom (monthly) and gistemp_tropics_annual_anom (yearly, 24S-24N).

## What it is
NASA GISS temperature departures from 1951-1980, global monthly and tropical yearly.

## How to read it
degC above normal. Dated the 15th of the month (monthly) or 1 July (yearly).

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Shows the global heat that El Nino adds on top of long-term warming.

## Limits
The yearly tropical value only appears once the year is complete.
