# Why the recommended spec matters

id: shopping_specs
short: For safety items the certification or strength decides whether the product protects you: a mask without N95/KN95/P2, a repellent without enough DEET or icaridin, or a filter coarser than 0.1 micron does not do the job.
category: prep
related: shopping_counterfeits, prep_health, prep_haze, prep_power, prep_water

## What it is
- **Masks**: certified respirators only: N95 (US NIOSH), KN95 (China GB 2626) or P2 (Australia/NZ) printed on the mask.
- **Water filter**: hollow-fibre membrane rated 0.1 micron removes bacteria and protozoa, not viruses or chemicals: still boil or chlorinate doubtful water.
- **Bleach for water**: sodium hypochlorite 5-6%, unscented; colour-safe bleach is not chlorine.
- **Repellent**: DEET 20-30% or icaridin (picaridin) about 20% on the label.
- **ORS**: pharmacy oral rehydration salts (WHO formula), not sports drinks.
- **Power station**: LiFePO4 battery, sized in Wh (phone charge about 15-20 Wh, USB fan 5-10 W, a fridge about 1 kWh per day).
- **Power bank**: 20,000 mAh is about 74 Wh; known brand with the Thai TIS mark.
- **Air purifier**: HEPA with a CADR / room size at least as big as the room you seal.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
In an emergency the item must work the first time; under-spec and fake products look the same in photos.

## Limits
Specs are general guidance, not a product endorsement; read the label of what you buy.
