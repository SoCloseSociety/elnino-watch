# Judging sea state from a beach or pier cam (ferry decisions)

id: webcams_sea_state
short: Turn what a beach or pier cam shows into a rough wind / wave estimate before a ferry.
applies_to: [webcams kind beach / pier, areas samui + ferry_route]

## What it is
A way to turn what you see on a coastal camera into a rough wind / wave estimate,
using the Beaufort scale sailors have used for 200 years.

## How to read it
Look at open water, not the shore break, and watch for 30-60 seconds:
- **Calm to light (Beaufort 0-2, < 7 knots)**: glassy or small ripples, no white.
  Ferries run normally.
- **Gentle to moderate (Bft 3-4, 7-16 kt, waves ~0.5-1 m)**: a few scattered
  whitecaps. Normal crossing, some spray on speedboats.
- **Fresh (Bft 5, 17-21 kt, ~2 m)**: many whitecaps everywhere, some spray.
  Speedboats and small boats may be cancelled; big car ferries usually run but it is
  rough.
- **Strong (Bft 6-7, 22-33 kt, 3-4 m)**: large waves with white foam crests, foam
  blown in streaks, spray. Expect cancellations; do not take a small boat.
- **Gale (Bft 8+)**: sea heaps up, long foam streaks. Boats stay in port.
Also check: rain curtains (grey vertical streaks) moving toward the camera = a squall
arriving in minutes; a hazy, milky horizon = poor visibility.

Which cams: north coast (Fisherman's Village, Big Buddha / Bangrak) for the Samui to
Phangan / Tao crossing; Bang Po (north-west) for the Nathon / Donsak lane; Koh Tao
Mae Haad for the far end; east coast (Crystal Bay, Lamai) for NE-monsoon swell.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Leaving the island means a boat or a Bangkok Airways flight. From about October to
December the north-east monsoon brings wind and waves to the Gulf of Thailand; the
Thai Meteorological Department then advises small boats to stay ashore when waves
reach 2-3 m. El Nino changes how wet and stormy that season is.

## Limits
- A camera shows the sea near shore. Mid-channel waves can be bigger.
- Lenses flatten waves; a wide angle makes 2 m look like 1 m.
- Night: nothing useful. Use the marine forecast instead.
- This is a sanity check. The official TMD warning and the ferry operator's decision
  always win.
