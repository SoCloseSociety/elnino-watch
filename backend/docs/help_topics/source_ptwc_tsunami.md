# PTWC tsunami messages

id: source_ptwc_tsunami
short: The latest message of NOAA's Pacific Tsunami Warning Center.

## What it is
PTWC (Honolulu) issues information statements, advisories, watches and warnings after significant earthquakes in the Pacific and the South China Sea.

## How to read it
Most messages are 'Information' and say there is no tsunami threat. The category is shown as a tag (information, advisory, watch, warning).

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Context only: the Gulf of Thailand is sheltered, and the Indian Ocean side is covered by the Indian Ocean Tsunami Warning System and Thailand's NDWC.

## Limits
Only the latest message is kept in the feed; the Pacific feed does not cover the Indian Ocean.
