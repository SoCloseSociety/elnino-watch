# Chart: change by 2050 per place
id: place_projection
short: Bars show the ensemble-mean change 2036-2050 vs 1991-2020 for each place; the thin range is the lowest and highest of the 6 models.
category: forecasts

## What it is

Read the bar for the average model answer and the range for the uncertainty. Precipitation changes are in % and often disagree in sign between models: treat them as uncertain.
