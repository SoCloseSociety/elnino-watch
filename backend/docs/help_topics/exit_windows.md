# Departure windows (next 7 days)

id: exit_windows
short: For each of the next 7 local days: are crossing conditions normal enough to leave by ferry?

## What it is
Each day combines that day's forecast waves and gusts, TMD Gulf wave bulletins until their end date and, for the first 3 days, a nearby cyclone.

## How to read it
ok = conditions look normal. caution = possible, expect delays or cancellations. blocked = ferries likely suspended. unknown = no forecast for that day. Pick the earliest ok day if you plan to leave.

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
Leaving on a calm day before a rough spell avoids being stuck when ferries stop.

## Limits
Wave forecasts beyond 3-4 days are less reliable. The operator's decision on the day is what counts.
