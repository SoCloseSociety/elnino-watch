# Where to buy (the "Buy" panel)

id: shopping_panel
short: Under each item you can buy: what to look for, prices seen on real shop pages (with date and link), search links for Lazada, Shopee and Thai retailers, and the stores on Samui that sell it.
category: prep

## What it is
A panel under each buyable checklist item (API: GET /api/shopping). It shows the recommended spec and why, real listings (Makro, HomePro, Global House, or marketplace sellers seen via the Priceza price-comparison site) with price, date checked and link, search links for Lazada and Shopee (English and Thai) and the retailers' sites, and the stores on Koh Samui that sell this kind of item (map search + a page confirming the store exists). Plans, documents and habits have no Buy panel.

## How to read it
The chip says how far to trust the price: **Price checked** (seen less than 60 days ago), **Re-check price** (older than 60 days: open the links first), **No verified price** (use the links).

## Why it matters for Koh Samui
When a storm is forecast, shelves on the island empty fast and ferries can stop. Knowing in advance what to buy, where, and roughly for how much lets you buy calmly and early.

## Limits
Prices are indicative and change; stock in island stores is not checked. Nothing is scraped live: the data is a curated file dated on the panel.
